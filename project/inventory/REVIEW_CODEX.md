## Six columns: accept with these clarifications

| Column | Clarification |
|---|---|
| Who supposes | Distinguish the attitude holder from whoever reports or quotes that attitude. A generic or hypothetical holder is valid; do not invent an actual episode. |
| What is supposed | State proposition P with its polarity. Believing not-P and not believing P are different. Allow multiple readings where the construction is unresolved. |
| Scope | Record layers together: quotation, condition, question, prohibition and negation may overlap. Specify which expression the negation governs. These are not mutually exclusive classes. |
| How the text presents its truth | Assess P at the stated discourse level. A quotation is not automatically endorsed; a question or prohibition does not mechanically assign truth or falsity. Keep open and not applicable. |
| Grounds | Separate what the text says the holder relied on from information given to the reader. No grounds stated does not mean the holder had no grounds. Distinguish grounds expressly denied from grounds merely unreported. |
| Note | Give verse and short Arabic evidence, context needed, confidence, and a serious alternative where applicable. Record any uncertainty about grouping. |

Read the whole verse and enough adjacent build context to resolve voice and scope; the inventory's short display windows are insufficient on their own. Record the context actually consulted. References to other partitions remain unavailable for this stage.
