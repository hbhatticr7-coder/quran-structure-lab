## Differences of substance

1. **What organises the sura.** My reader: mercy shown as the giving of children and kin, leading to the conclusion that the giver of children does not himself take one. Codex's reader: an order that removes supposed guarantees one by one (requested offspring, unusual birth, prophetic kinship, succession, wealth, divine parentage) while keeping the gifts. These are compatible, and Codex's covers verses 59 and 73 to 80 better. Both give the same serious alternative, a joined series of units, and both cite the unintroduced voice at verse 64 for it.
2. **Where order does work.** Both readers, independently, name the same case: the reply to Zakariyya at verse 9 returns as the answer to the doubter at verse 67, so the later argument uses a scene already told. Both also follow the inheritance words through verses 6, 40, 63 and 80.
3. **Verse 78.** Both read the two questions as a test of the boaster's grounds and not only an announcement of his fate.
4. **Verse 71, "none of you but will come to it".** My reader took "you" as everyone, with medium confidence. Codex's left the membership open. Codex's caution is better supported: the second person appears without a marked change of audience, and verse 72 does not settle it.
5. **Speakers.** At verses 7 and 12 my reader wrote `God quoted` and Codex's `open`. At 64 my reader gave the level as quoted and Codex's as open. Both leave 9, 24 and 36 open. Nothing in my essay rests on these.
6. **A remembered claim.** My reader recalled, without checking, that some scholars treat verses 34 to 40 as a later addition on the ground of rhyme. Codex's reader did not raise it. It is unverified and no argument here depends on it.

## What both accounts hold after the exchange

- The two birth stories are built in parallel, with the same question and the same answer.
- Unusual birth, lineage and prosperity are each refused as proof of standing.
- Verse 83, where devils are sent upon the deniers, raises a question of responsibility that the sura does not answer. Neither reader resolves it.
- Neither reader verifies anything outside the text.
