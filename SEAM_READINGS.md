# Seam readings

For each boundary between two suras, a reader saw a summary of the close and summaries of five candidate openings (the next sura and its four nearest other neighbours) and ranked them. This file gives the connection the reader saw between the close and its first choice.

**Read this with care.** The readers were language models. A check showed they can identify suras from these summaries, so their choices are not blind and prove nothing about the order (see `RESULTS.md`, batch 19). Where the first choice was the true next sura, the reason is a candidate link of meaning to check against the text. Where it was another sura, the reason shows how plausible a link to a neighbour can also look.
