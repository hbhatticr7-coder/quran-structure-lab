Many earlier prophets were mocked, and stronger peoples destroyed. Asked who created heavens and earth, they would name the mighty, knowing one, who made the earth a cradle with roads.
C: This scripture comes from God, mighty and knowing, who forgives sin, accepts repentance, punishes severely and gives abundantly. Only deniers dispute his signs; do not be fooled by their free movement through the land. Earlier peoples also denied, plotted to seize their messengers and argued with falsehood against truth, and were seized. So deniers are destined for fire.
D: By the clear scripture: sent down on a blessed night as warning, when every wise matter is decided by divine command, a mercy from the Lord of heavens and earth, the only god, who gives life and death, Lord of you and your forefathers. Yet they play in doubt. So watch for the day the sky brings visible smoke.
E: From the Merciful, a scripture of detailed verses in its hearers' language, giving good news and warning, yet most turn away, saying their hearts are covered, their ears deaf, a veil between; act, as we will. Reply: I am only human, inspired that your god is one; go straight to Him. Woe to idolaters withholding alms and denying the hereafter.

CLOSE: Have they not travelled and seen the end of those before them, more numerous and stronger? Their gains did not help. When messengers brought clear proofs they exulted in their knowledge, and what they mocked engulfed them. Seeing Our might, they professed belief in God alone, but belief then did not help. Such is God's way; the deniers lost.
A: By the reminding recitation: deniers are proud and defiant. Many earlier generations perished, crying out too late. Amazed that one of them warns, they call him a lying sorcerer: has he made the gods one god? Their chiefs say: hold to your gods; this is a scheme, unheard of, invented; why him among us? === ITEM S482 ===
CLOSE: Had this scripture come down on a mountain, you would see it humbled and split from fear of God, a comparison for reflection. He is God, the only god: knower of hidden and seen, merciful, king, holy, giver of peace and security, guardian, mighty, compeller, supreme, creator, maker, shaper, bearing the finest names, glorified by heaven and earth.
A: Everything glorifies God, the holy and wise king. He raised among an unlettered people a messenger from themselves to recite his signs, purify them and teach scripture and wisdom, though they had been in error, and for others yet to join them. Those given an earlier law who failed to carry it are like a donkey loaded with books.
B: God has heard the woman who argued about her husband and complained to him. Men who declare their wives to be like their mothers speak falsely; only those who bore them are mothers. If they retract, they must free a slave before touching their wives, or fast two consecutive months, or feed sixty poor people. These are God's limits.
C: Everything in heaven and earth glorifies God. Believers are rebuked for saying what they do not do, which God hates; he loves those who fight for him in ranks like a solid wall. One prophet asked his people why they hurt him; another confirmed earlier law and foretold a named messenger after him, but was called a sorcerer.
D: Believers must not befriend God's enemy and theirs, who rejected the truth and expelled the messenger and them for believing. If you set out striving for Me, why secretly show them affection, when I know what you hide and reveal? Doing so is straying. Given the upper hand, they would harm you by hand and tongue and wish you disbelieved.
E: Everything in heaven and earth glorifies God, who owns their kingdom, gives life and death and can do all things. He is first and last, evident and hidden. He made heavens and earth in six days and took the throne; he knows what enters and leaves earth and sky, and is with people wherever they are.

=== ITEM S112 ===
CLOSE: Say: truth has come; falsehood neither begins nor restores anything. If I stray it is my loss; if guided, it is by my Lord's revelation. See them terrified, with no escape, seized from close by, saying they believe; but how can they reach it from afar, having denied before? They are barred from what they desire, like doubters before them.
A: A prophet is told to fear God, not obey deniers and hypocrites, follow revelation and trust God as guardian. God has not put two hearts in any man, nor made wives you liken to your mothers into your mothers, nor made those you call sons your real sons. Those are mere words of your mouths; God speaks truth and guides.
B: By the wise recitation, the listener is a messenger on a straight path, sent to warn a heedless people whose fathers went unwarned. Most will not believe: their necks are shackled to the chin, and barriers before and behind blind them. Warning helps only one who follows the reminder and fears the Merciful unseen; promise him forgiveness and reward.
C: This scripture is undoubtedly from the Lord of the worlds; not invented but truth, to warn a people never warned before. God created heavens, earth and all between in six days, then took the throne; no other protector or intercessor exists. He directs affairs from heaven to earth; they rise to him in a day like a thousand years.
D: Praise to God, originator of heavens and earth, who makes angels messengers with two, three or four wings, adding to creation as He wills. Mercy God opens none can withhold; what He withholds none can release. People, remember God's favour: does any creator besides God provide for you from sky and earth? No god but Him; so why turn away?
E: Oaths by those ranged in rows, those who drive back, those reciting a reminder: your God is one, Lord of heavens, earth and the sunrises. The nearest sky is adorned with stars and guarded from devils, who cannot overhear the high assembly and are pelted and chased by piercing flame. Ask: are they harder to create, made from sticky clay?
