Accusers of chaste women who lack four witnesses get eighty lashes and permanent rejection as witnesses.
D: People's reckoning nears while they turn away heedless, hearing each new reminder while playing. Wrongdoers whisper: is he not merely human like you; will you accept sorcery open-eyed? He says his Lord knows every word in heaven and earth. They call it muddled dreams, invention, poetry, and demand a sign like earlier messengers'. No town destroyed earlier believed; will they?
E: This recitation came not to distress you but to remind the fearful, from earth and heaven's Creator, the Merciful enthroned, owning all above, below and underground, who knows secrets and things more hidden, with the finest names. Then a story: a man saw a fire and told his family to wait while he sought a brand or guidance.

=== ITEM R167 ===
CLOSE: An oath by time: humankind is surely in a state of loss, except those who believe, do good deeds, urge one another to truth, and urge one another to patience.
A: Rivalry in piling up more distracts you until you visit the graves. No, you will come to know; again, you will come to know. If only you knew with certainty: you will surely see the blazing fire, see it with the eye of certainty, and then on that day be questioned about the comforts you enjoyed.
B: The striking calamity: what is it, and what will make you grasp it? A day when people are like scattered moths and the mountains like carded wool. Whoever's scales weigh heavy will have a pleasing life; whoever's scales are light, the pit is his mother. And what is that? A raging fire.
C: The listener is asked whether he has not seen how his Lord dealt with the army that came with an elephant: he made their scheme go astray, sent flocks of birds against them that pelted them with stones of baked clay, and left them like chewed-up straw.
D: For the accustomed security of a tribe, their being accustomed to the winter and summer journeys, let them worship the Lord of this house, who has fed them against hunger and made them safe from fear.
E: Woe to every backbiter and fault-finder who gathers wealth and counts it, thinking his wealth will make him last forever. No: he will be flung into the crusher. And what is the crusher? It is God's kindled fire that reaches up over the hearts, closed in upon them in extended columns.

=== ITEM R139 ===
CLOSE: Whether We show you what We threaten or take you first, your duty is only to deliver; the reckoning is Ours. Do they not see We shrink the land from its edges? None reverses God's judgment. Earlier people schemed, but all scheming is God's. They say you are no messenger; say: God suffices as witness, and whoever knows scripture.
A: This scripture was sent so the listener may bring people from darkness into light, onto the path of God, owner of everything. Woe to deniers who prefer this life to the next, block God's way and seek to bend it. Every messenger spoke his own people's language to make things clear; God guides or misleads as he wills.
B: These are verses of a clear scripture. Deniers may one day wish they had submitted; leave them to eat, enjoy and be lulled by hope. No town perished except at its fixed term. They call the recipient of the reminder mad and demand angels; angels come only with final truth, leaving no respite. God sent the reminder and guards it.
C: God's command is coming, so do not hurry it; He is far above their partner-gods. He sends angels with the spirit to chosen servants to warn that He alone is god. He made heavens and earth, and man from a drop, who openly disputes. Livestock give warmth, food, beauty at evening and morning, and carry loads to distant lands.
D: A scripture of verses perfected then detailed from one wise and aware: worship only God. The speaker warns and brings good news; seek forgiveness and repent to enjoy good provision for a term, or fear a great day. All return to God. Some fold their chests to hide from him, but even under their garments he knows their secrets.
E: These are verses of a clear scripture in its hearers' language; the best of stories is told to one formerly unaware. A son tells his father he dreamed eleven stars, sun and moon bowing to him. The father warns him not to tell his brothers lest they plot; God will choose him, teach him interpretation and complete His favour.

=== ITEM R593 ===
CLOSE: They ask when this promise will come. Say: knowledge is with God; I am only a plain warner. When they see it near, deniers' faces fall. Say: whether God destroys me and my companions or shows us mercy, who will shield deniers from punishment? We trust the Merciful. If your water sank away, who would bring you flowing water?
A: The prophet is told that divorce must observe the waiting period, counted carefully. Wives are not to be expelled from their homes, nor leave, unless guilty of open indecency; God may bring a change afterward. When the term ends, keep them or part from them honorably, before two just witnesses. Whoever is mindful of God is given a way out.
B: The inevitable: what is it? Two peoples denied the striking calamity. One perished by an overwhelming blast, the other by a furious wind across seven nights, eight days, felling them like hollow palm trunks, no remnant. A ruler, his predecessors and the overturned towns defied their Lord's messenger and were seized hard. When water overflowed, We carried you by ship.
C: A prophet is asked why he forbids himself what God allowed, to please his wives; oaths may be released. He confided in a wife; she disclosed it; God informed him; he raised part, saying the All-Knowing told him. Two women should repent, their hearts swerved; if they unite against him, God, an angel, righteous believers and angels support him.
D: Someone asked about a punishment sure to befall deniers, unstoppable, from God of the ascents, to whom angels and the spirit rise in a day of fifty thousand years. Be gracefully patient; they see it far, We see it near. That day sky resembles molten metal, mountains like wool, friend ignores friend; the guilty would ransom himself with his children.
E: By the pen and what they write: you are not mad, by your Lord's grace; you have unending reward and great character. You and they will see who is afflicted. Do not yield to deniers, who want mutual compromise, nor obey any contemptible oath-monger, slanderer, tale-bearer, hinderer of good, sinful, coarse and base, just because he has wealth and sons.

=== ITEM R376 ===
CLOSE: Arrogance and evil scheming, which traps only its authors. Do they await anything but the ancients' fate? God's way never changes or shifts. Have they not travelled and seen how stronger predecessors ended? Nothing escapes God. Were people punished for their deeds, no creature would remain on earth; instead he defers them to a fixed term, seeing his servants.
A: Oaths by those ranged in rows, those who drive back, those reciting a reminder: your God is one, Lord of heavens, earth and the sunrises. The nearest sky is adorned with stars and guarded from devils, who cannot overhear the high assembly and are pelted and chased by piercing flame. Ask: are they harder to create, made from sticky clay?
B: By the reminding recitation: deniers are proud and defiant. Many earlier generations perished, crying out too late. Amazed that one of them warns, they call him a lying sorcerer: has he made the gods one god? Their chiefs say: hold to your gods; this is a scheme, unheard of, invented; why him among us? They have not yet tasted punishment.
C: A prophet is told to fear God, not obey deniers and hypocrites, follow revelation and trust God as guardian. God has not put two hearts in any man, nor made wives you liken to your mothers into your mothers, nor made those you call sons your real sons. Those are mere words of your mouths; God speaks truth and guides.
D: Praise God, owner of all in heaven and earth, praised also in the hereafter. He knows what enters and leaves the earth, what descends from and ascends to the sky. Deniers say the hour will not come; swear that it will. Not an atom's weight, smaller or larger, escapes his clear record, so believers who do good are rewarded.
E: By the wise recitation, the listener is a messenger on a straight path, sent to warn a heedless people whose fathers went unwarned. Most will not believe: their necks are shackled to the chin, and barriers before and behind blind them. Warning helps only one who follows the reminder and fears the Merciful unseen; promise him forgiveness and reward.
