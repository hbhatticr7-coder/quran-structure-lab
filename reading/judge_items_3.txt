=== ITEM S877 ===
CLOSE: For diseased hearts it adds filth to filth. Tested once or twice yearly, they neither repent nor take heed. When a chapter is sent down they glance at each other, ask if anyone sees them, and slip away. A messenger from among yourselves has come, pained by your hardship, kind to believers. If they turn away, say: God suffices me.
A: Verses of the wise scripture. Is it strange one of them was inspired to warn and promise believers sure standing? Deniers say: a sorcerer. Your Lord made heavens and earth in six days, took the throne, directs all; none intercedes without His leave. All return to Him; He begins and repeats creation to reward the righteous; deniers get scalding drink.
B: A scripture is sent to the listener, who should feel no unease about it, to warn and remind. People should follow it and no other patrons. Many towns were destroyed at night or midday rest, crying only that they had done wrong. Messengers and their audiences will be questioned; deeds will be weighed, and heavy scales bring success.
C: These are verses of a clear scripture in its hearers' language; the best of stories is told to one formerly unaware. A son tells his father he dreamed eleven stars, sun and moon bowing to him. The father warns him not to tell his brothers lest they plot; God will choose him, teach him interpretation and complete His favour.
D: A scripture of verses perfected then detailed from one wise and aware: worship only God. The speaker warns and brings good news; seek forgiveness and repent to enjoy good provision for a term, or fear a great day. All return to God. Some fold their chests to hide from him, but even under their garments he knows their secrets.
E: Asked about war spoils, say they belong to God and the messenger, so fear God, mend relations and obey. True believers' hearts tremble at God's mention and their faith grows on hearing His verses; they trust Him, pray and spend, gaining ranks, forgiveness and generous provision. Likewise your Lord rightly brought you from your home, though some believers were unwilling.

=== ITEM S761 ===
CLOSE: Have they a god other than God? He is above such partners. Seeing a piece of sky fall, they would call it heaped clouds. Leave them until the day they are thunderstruck, their plotting useless; wrongdoers face nearer punishment too. Patiently await your Lord's judgment, you are in our sight; praise him on rising, at night, as stars fade.
A: By the setting star, their companion neither strays nor speaks from whim: it is revelation, taught by a mighty one who stood on the highest horizon, drew within two bow-lengths and revealed. His heart did not falsify what he saw. He saw him again by the farthest boundary tree, near the garden of refuge, when something enveloped the tree.
B: The Merciful taught the recitation, created man and taught him speech. Sun and moon follow a reckoning; plants and trees bow. He raised the sky and set the balance: weigh justly, do not cheat. He spread the earth for creatures: fruit, sheathed palms, husked grain and herbs. Which favors will the two addressed deny? Man came from potter's clay.
C: By the glorious recitation: they marvel that one of their own came warning, saying: once dead and dust, a far-fetched return! We know what earth consumes of them and keep a record. Denying truth, they are confused. Have they not seen the sky above, built and adorned without rift, and the earth spread, set with mountains, growing every lovely kind?
D: The hour nears and the moon has split. Yet seeing a sign they turn away, call it lasting sorcery and follow their whims. Deterring tidings and far-reaching wisdom have come, but warnings fail. Turn from them. When the caller summons to something dreadful, they leave graves with lowered eyes like scattered locusts, hurrying to him; deniers say: a hard day.
E: Oaths by those that scatter, bear loads, glide easily and apportion a command: the promise is true and judgement will come. By the sky of woven paths, your views conflict. Cursed are heedless guessers asking when judgement comes: when they are tried over fire and told to taste what they hurried. The mindful, former good-doers, are among gardens and springs.

=== ITEM S989 ===
CLOSE: Read: your Lord is most generous, who taught by the pen what man did not know. Yet man transgresses, thinking himself self-sufficient. Consider one who forbids a servant to pray: does he not know God sees? If he does not stop he will be dragged by his lying forelock. Do not obey him; bow down and draw near.
A: When the earth is shaken violently and throws out its burdens, man will ask what is wrong with it. That day it will tell its news, because its Lord has inspired it. People will come out in scattered groups to be shown their deeds: whoever did an atom's weight of good will see it, and likewise of evil.
B: By the fig and the olive, by a mountain and by this secure city: man was created in the finest form, then brought down to the lowest of the low, except those who believe and do good, whose reward never ends. What then can make anyone deny the judgment? Is God not the wisest of judges?
C: The listener is reminded that his chest was opened, the burden that weighed down his back was lifted, and his reputation raised. He is told twice that ease comes with hardship. So when he is free he should keep striving and direct his longing to his Lord.
D: Deniers among scripture-holders and idolaters would not desist until clear proof came: a messenger from God reciting purified pages of upright writings. Scripture-holders split only after that proof, though commanded only to worship God sincerely, pray and give alms, which is the upright religion. Such deniers stay forever in hellfire, the worst of creatures.
E: We sent it down on the night of decree. What will make you grasp what the night of decree is? It is better than a thousand months. In it the angels and the spirit descend, by their Lord's permission, with every matter. It is peace until the rising of dawn.
