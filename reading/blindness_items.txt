[B735] He gives sons and daughters together, or makes someone barren. God addresses a human only by inspiration, from behind a veil, or through a messenger. So we sent you a spirit; you knew neither book nor faith, but we made it a guiding light. You guide to the straight path of God, to whom all matters return.

[B894] O human, what deceived you about your generous Lord, who created, shaped and balanced you in whatever form He willed? Yet you deny the judgment, though noble recording guardians know what you do. The righteous will be in bliss, the wicked in blazing fire. What is judgment day? A day no soul can help another, and command is God's alone.

[B251] Those mindful of their Lord have gardens with flowing rivers forever, as God's hospitality. Some holders of earlier scripture believe in God, in what was sent to you and to them, humbly, not selling God's signs cheaply; their reward awaits, and God reckons swiftly. Believers: be patient, outlast others in patience, stand guard, and fear God to succeed.

[B306] Read: your Lord is most generous, who taught by the pen what man did not know. Yet man transgresses, thinking himself self-sufficient. Consider one who forbids a servant to pray: does he not know God sees? If he does not stop he will be dragged by his lying forelock. Do not obey him; bow down and draw near.

[B162] Oaths by sun, moon, day, night, sky, earth, and the soul inspired with its wickedness and piety: whoever purifies it succeeds, whoever buries it fails. A people denied in arrogance; their most wretched rose, the messenger warned about God's she-camel and her drink, they hamstrung her, and their Lord crushed and leveled them, fearing no consequence.

[B710] Set up no other god; I am a plain warner. Earlier messengers too were called sorcerer or madman, as if by agreement. Turn away blameless, yet remind, which benefits believers. I created unseen beings and humans only to worship me, wanting neither provision nor food from them. Wrongdoers get their predecessors' share; woe to unbelievers on their promised day.

[B946] Set your face to the upright religion; do not call on what can neither help nor harm. Harm from God none can remove but He; His bounty none can turn back. Say: truth has come from your Lord; whoever is guided or strays does so for or against himself; I am not your guardian. Be patient until God judges.

[B371] You make denial your portion. When a dying soul reaches the throat while you watch, and we are nearer though unseen, why not bring it back if you owe no account? One brought near gets rest, fragrance, a blissful garden; one of the right hand, peace; a straying denier, scalding water and hellfire. This is certain; glorify your Lord.

[B913] They say they stayed a day or less; ask those who keep count. He says it was brief, had they known. Did you think we made you pointlessly, never to return? Exalted is God, the true king, lord of the noble throne. Whoever invokes another god without proof answers to his Lord. Say: Lord, forgive and have mercy.

[B403] Scalding water is poured on a man's head as he is mocked as mighty and noble; this they had doubted. The mindful are secure among gardens and springs, in silk and brocade, face to face, paired with wide-eyed companions, calling for any fruit, tasting no second death, spared hell. It was eased in your tongue; wait, as they wait.

[B800] They ask when this promise will come. Say: knowledge is with God; I am only a plain warner. When they see it near, deniers' faces fall. Say: whether God destroys me and my companions or shows us mercy, who will shield deniers from punishment? We trust the Merciful. If your water sank away, who would bring you flowing water?

[B703] An earlier scripture records that righteous servants will inherit the earth. You were sent only as mercy to the worlds. Say: your god is one God; will you submit? If they turn away: I have warned you all alike, not knowing whether the promise is near or far; perhaps it is a trial. He asks his Lord to judge truly.

[B362] If wives defect to unbelievers and your turn comes, repay their husbands' outlay. Believing women pledge: no partners for God, no theft, adultery, child-killing, invented slander or disobedience in what is right; accept and ask forgiveness for them. Do not ally with people under God's anger, who despair of the hereafter as unbelievers despair of the buried.

[B683] Those who die unbelieving after blocking God's way are never forgiven. Do not weaken and seek peace while uppermost. This life is play; believe and he rewards you without demanding your wealth, which would expose your grudges. Asked to spend, some are stingy, only toward themselves; God is rich, you are poor. Turn away and he replaces you.

[B592] When God's help and victory come and you see people entering God's religion in crowds, glorify your Lord with praise and ask His forgiveness; He is ever accepting of repentance.

[B185] The messenger and the believers believe in God, his angels, books and messengers, without distinguishing among messengers, hearing and obeying. God burdens no soul beyond its capacity; each bears what it earned. Lord, excuse our forgetting and mistakes, do not load us like earlier peoples or beyond our strength; pardon, forgive, have mercy; protector, help us against unbelievers.

[B228] Faces content with their effort, in a high garden free of idle talk: a flowing spring, raised couches, set cups, rowed cushions, spread carpets. Will they not look at how camels were made, the sky raised, mountains fixed, earth spread? Remind; you are no controller over them. Whoever turns away God punishes greatly; they return to us for reckoning.

[B187] My task is only delivering God's messages; whoever disobeys has the fire forever. When they see what is promised they will know whose helpers are weaker and fewer. Say: I do not know if it is near or far. He reveals the unseen only to a chosen messenger, whom He surrounds with watchers to ensure delivery; He counts everything.

[B853] After other messengers came a prophet, a woman's son, with a scripture, and we put compassion and mercy in his followers' hearts. Monastic life they invented, seeking God's approval, yet kept it poorly; many disobeyed. Believers, fear God and trust his messenger: he gives double mercy, a light to walk by and forgiveness, so scripture-holders know bounty is God's alone.

[B390] Did we not open up your chest, lift from you the burden that weighed down your back, and raise your renown? Truly with hardship comes ease; truly with hardship comes ease. So when you are free, keep striving, and direct your longing to your Lord.

[B146] We have given you abundance, so pray to your Lord and offer sacrifice. It is the one who hates you who is cut off and left without a future.

[B206] God sees the addressee when he stands and moves among those who bow down. Devils descend on every sinful liar, passing on what they overhear, mostly lies. Poets are followed by the misguided, roam every valley and say what they do not do, except believers who do good and defend themselves after being wronged. Wrongdoers will learn their fate.

[B746] True believers never leave a gathering with the messenger without his leave; grant it as you wish, asking forgiveness for them. Do not treat his summons like calls among yourselves. God knows who slips away under cover; defiers of his order should fear trial or painful punishment. God owns everything, knows your state, and will recount their deeds.

[B916] Do not think God will break His promise to His messengers. On the day earth and heavens are changed into others, all stand before God the One. You see the guilty chained, in garments of pitch, fire covering their faces, each soul repaid. This is a message to warn mankind that He is one God and make the thoughtful reflect.

[B330] A curse on a certain man: may his hands perish, and may he perish. His wealth and all he gained are no use to him. He will burn in a flaming fire, and so will his wife, the carrier of firewood, with a rope of twisted fibre around her neck.

[B526] Whoever brings a good deed gets better and is safe from that day's terror; whoever brings evil is thrown face-down into fire. I am ordered to worship the Lord who made this city sacred, to submit, and recite the scripture. The guided benefit themselves; I only warn the stray. God will show signs you will recognize; he misses nothing.

[B100] God sent his messenger with guidance and true religion to prevail over all religion. He and his companions are firm against unbelievers, merciful among themselves, bowing and prostrating, faces marked by it. One scripture pictures them so; another as a seed that sprouts, thickens and stands on its stalk, delighting sowers, enraging unbelievers. Forgiveness and great reward are promised.

[B152] We sent it down on the night of decree. What could tell you what that night is? It is better than a thousand months: in it the angels and the spirit descend by their Lord's leave with every command, and it is peace until the break of dawn.

[B587] Believers, when prayer is called on the day of assembly, hurry to remember God and leave off trading. When the prayer ends, disperse, seek God's bounty and remember him often. Yet when they see a sale or amusement they rush to it and leave you standing. Say: what God has is better than both; he is the best provider.

[B814] Your strivings differ. The mindful giver who affirms the best we ease toward ease; the self-sufficient miser who denies it we ease toward hardship, his wealth useless when he falls. Guidance and both worlds are ours. Fire awaits the wretched denier; spared is the devout who gives wealth for purity, seeking only his Lord's face; he will be satisfied.

[B653] Believers who do good have gardens of paradise as lodging, never wishing to leave. Say: were the sea ink for my Lord's words it would run dry first, even with another sea added. Say: I am only a human like you, told your god is one; whoever hopes to meet his Lord should do good and worship none besides.

[B377] They say that on returning to the city the mightier will drive out the lowlier; but might belongs to God, His messenger and the believers. Believers: let not wealth and children distract you from remembering God. Spend before death comes and one begs a short delay to give charity. God delays no soul once its term arrives.

[B474] Leave them to plunge and play until their promised day. He is God in heaven and on earth, owner of all between, who knows the hour. Those they invoke besides Him cannot intercede, except knowing witnesses to truth. Asked who created them they would say God. At the complaint that these people will not believe: turn away and say peace.

[B264] A being boasts superiority: fire-made, the other clay. God expels and curses him until judgment day, yet grants reprieve until a set time. He vows to mislead all but God's chosen servants; God vows to fill hell with him and his followers. Say: I ask no fee and do not pretend; this reminder's truth you will learn.

[B102] Your Lord will judge their disputes on the day of rising. Is it no lesson how many generations were destroyed, in whose dwellings they walk? Do they not see water driven to barren land, bringing crops? They ask when the decision will come. Say: that day belief will not help deniers. Turn away and wait; they wait too.

[B721] Bear patiently what they say; glorify your Lord before sunrise and sunset, at night and after prostrations. Listen for the day the caller calls from nearby and they hear the cry: the day of coming forth, when the earth splits and they hurry out. You are no tyrant over them; remind by this scripture whoever fears My warning.

[B213] A command to say: I seek refuge with the Lord of daybreak from the harm of what he created, from the harm of darkness when it settles in, from the harm of those who blow on knots, and from the harm of an envier when he envies.

[B241] Those who believed, emigrated and strove, and those who sheltered and helped them, are one another's allies. Believers who have not emigrated get no protection until they do, yet must be helped in religion unless against a people under treaty. Deniers ally with each other. Later emigrants belong too, and blood relatives have first claim on each other.

[B728] Why do the deniers rush toward you in clusters from right and left? Does each hope to enter a garden of bliss? No; they know what We made them from. We can replace them with better. Leave them to plunge and play until their promised day, when they hurry from graves as if racing to a marker, eyes downcast, humiliated.

[B820] People are told to fear a day when no father can stand in for his child, nor child for father. God's promise is true; let neither worldly life nor the deceiver delude you. God alone knows the hour, sends rain and knows what wombs hold; no soul knows what it will earn tomorrow or where it will die.
